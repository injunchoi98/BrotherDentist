# Official logo sources

These assets are production-facing manufacturer marks downloaded from each brand's official website. The `.svg` files preserve the official vector originals and the matching `.png` files are local rasterizations used by the page for consistent browser delivery. They are used only as visual examples of dental material and equipment brands; inclusion does not state a partnership or endorsement.

- `osstem.png` — OSSTEM IMPLANT, official mobile site asset (`mns.osstem.com`)
- `dentium.svg` — Dentium, `https://www.dentium.com/images/common/logo.svg`
- `dio.svg` — DIO Implant, `https://www.dioimplant.com/assets/images/common/logo.svg`
- `megagen.svg` — MegaGen, `https://imegagen.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/logo_b_new-1.svg`
- `straumann.svg` — Straumann, `https://shop.straumann.com/straumann-brand-logos/straumann.svg`
- `dentsply-sirona.svg` — Dentsply Sirona, official digital asset manager
- `ivoclar.svg` — Ivoclar, `https://www.ivoclar.com/SYSTEM/ivoclar-logo.svg`
- `gc.png` — GC, official GC website asset
- `3m.svg` — 3M, `https://news.3m.com/images/unicorn/Logo.svg`
- `snu.png` — 서울대학교 정장, official university symbol page (`https://www.snu.ac.kr/about/symbol/ui`)
- `snu-emblem.png` — 위 공식 정장 파일에서 단일 청색 정장만 분리한 로컬 표시용 파생본
- `yonsei.png` — 연세대학교 심벌마크, official university UI page (`https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/sc/336/subview.do`)
- `khu.png` — 경희대학교 signature, official university homepage header asset (`https://www.khu.ac.kr/kor/user/main/view.do`)

The university marks correspond to education or training records already presented in the medical staff profiles. They do not state a university partnership or endorsement.
